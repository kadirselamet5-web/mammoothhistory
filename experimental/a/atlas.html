        Method

        How to read this map

        The dataset

        This atlas presents all {{TOTAL}} data rows in {{SOURCE_SHEET}} of {{SOURCE_FILE}}, the Lab’s current soft-tissue dataset (workbook dated {{SOURCE_DATE}}; map built {{BUILD_DATE}}). It is not a comprehensive inventory of preserved megafauna.

        The workbook contains {{TOTAL}} rows and {{UNIQUE_IDS}} distinct Specimen_id values; {{MAPPED}} rows carry usable coordinates and {{UNMAPPED}} do not. The ID numbers are temporary checks for the Lab’s work in progress, not permanent specimen identifiers or citation keys. The {{TAXA_COUNT}} taxon groups follow the workbook’s Species_type column, including Cave Lion/Felid, which also contains Homotherium latidens. The Specimen_type_list sheet also lists Caribou and Elk, for which no soft-tissue records have been entered yet.

        {{CHANGES}}

        Coordinate provenance

        Each row’s Location Provenance cell records how the find coordinate was obtained. The labels are reproduced as written:

          {{PROVENANCE_LIST}}

        Source-stated coordinates are drawn as solid points, inferred and historical-map positions with a dashed ring, and regional placeholders or unlocated finds as hollow points. A placeholder marks a region in which the find was reported, not a find spot, so clusters of hollow points can share one coordinate. No accuracy class is inferred from the number of decimal places, and no coordinate has been moved.

        Source wording and display fields

        Original cell values are retained for every source column. Spaces and underscores are normalized for readable names; the embedded source values retain the original spelling. Specimen-age text is reproduced in the wording and notation recorded from the sources. Blank cells, not_available, not_applicable and ? remain distinct source values. Material tags are derived from the Parts_found descriptions; phrases that describe missing parts are ignored when tagging, and “Whole / near-whole” follows the Complete? field. Text found in header-less workbook columns is shown in the record as a workbook note.

        Discovery years and specimen ages

        Discovery sites use recorded find years, spanning {{YEAR_SPAN}} in this workbook; collection year is the fallback where needed. Specimen ages use the text in Age_BP_radiocarbon and do not inherit the discovery-year filter. Ranges and stated ± uncertainty are retained as intervals, arrows show lower limits, and alternative values are evaluated separately. Digit groups separated by a space, comma or period (“29 500”, “34, 450”, “31.700”) are read as thousands. Nothing is translated, standardized or recalibrated; BP conventionally refers to years before 1950, but calibration and notation differ between sources.

        Source values to review

        These notes flag possible issues while retaining the workbook values. They also appear in the affected records.

          {{REVIEW_LIST}}

        Individual localities

        Each specimen remains an individual point. When symbols overlap, the interface separates them on screen and draws fine leader lines or globe stems back to their coordinate anchors. This is a legibility adjustment, not a new locality or a measure of spatial uncertainty. The flat map uses a rectangular 28–86°N frame with separate horizontal and vertical scaling; use it to locate records, not to measure area or distance. The polar globe keeps the same coastline geometry; only the facing surface is selectable.

        Using the atlas

        Taxon and provenance choices are inclusive; material filters match any selected tag. Counts reflect the other active filters. Discovery bars select a decade. Every map point opens its individual record; the age plot also opens records. Ctrl/⌘ + scroll zooms the map; ordinary scrolling moves the page. All data, coastline geometry and the globe renderer are embedded in this HTML.
