Catalogue notes

Method, conventions and citation for edition {{SOURCE_DATE}} (built {{BUILD_DATE}}).

Scope

This catalogue presents all {{TOTAL}} data rows in {{SOURCE_SHEET}} of {{SOURCE_FILE}}, the Lab’s current soft-tissue dataset. It shows documented find sites and discovery years alongside the specimen ages reported in the sources, and labels how each find position was obtained. It is not a comprehensive inventory of preserved megafauna.

Record identity

The workbook contains {{TOTAL}} rows and {{UNIQUE_IDS}} distinct Specimen_id values; {{MAPPED}} rows carry usable coordinates and {{UNMAPPED}} do not. ID numbers are temporary checks for the Lab’s work in progress, not permanent specimen identifiers or citation keys. The {{TAXA_COUNT}} taxon groups follow the workbook’s Species_type column, including Cave Lion/Felid, which also contains Homotherium latidens. The workbook’s Specimen_type_list sheet also lists Caribou and Elk, for which no soft-tissue records have been entered yet.

{{CHANGES}}

Coordinate provenance

Each row’s Location Provenance cell records how the find coordinate was obtained. The labels are reproduced as written:

    {{PROVENANCE_LIST}}

Source-stated coordinates are drawn as solid points, inferred and historical-map positions with a dashed ring, and regional placeholders or unlocated finds as hollow points. A placeholder marks a region in which the find was reported, not a find spot, so clusters of hollow points can share one coordinate. No accuracy class is inferred from the number of decimal places, and no coordinate has been moved.

Source wording and display fields

Original cell values are retained for every source column. Spaces and underscores are normalized for readable names; the embedded source values keep the original spelling. Specimen-age text is reproduced in the wording and notation recorded from the sources. Blank cells, not_available, not_applicable and ? remain distinct source values. Material tags are derived from the Parts_found descriptions; phrases that describe missing parts are ignored when tagging, and “Whole / near-whole” follows the workbook’s Complete? field. Text found in header-less workbook columns is shown in the record as a workbook note.

Map positions

Each specimen remains an individual point. When symbols overlap, the interface separates them on screen and draws fine leader lines or globe stems back to their coordinate anchors. This is a legibility adjustment, not a new locality or a measure of spatial uncertainty. The flat map uses a rectangular 28–86°N frame with separate horizontal and vertical scaling; use it to locate records, not to measure area or distance. Coastline geometry comes from the original HTML; its source attribution was not supplied.

Discovery years and specimen ages

Discovery sites uses recorded find years, spanning {{YEAR_SPAN}} in this workbook, with the collection year as a fallback. Specimen ages uses the text in Age_BP_radiocarbon and does not inherit the discovery-year filter. Ranges and stated ± uncertainty are retained as intervals, arrows show lower limits, and alternative values are evaluated separately. Digit groups separated by a space, comma or period (“29 500”, “34, 450”, “31.700”) are read as thousands. Nothing is translated, standardized or recalibrated: BP conventionally refers to years before 1950, but calibration and notation differ between sources, and the numeric display does not make these timescales equivalent.

The dataset link and DOI will be added when publication is complete; “See dataset” will open that publication. Published and archival reference keys are reproduced from the workbook; the published dataset will provide the authoritative citation and reuse terms.

Using the catalogue

Taxon and provenance choices are inclusive; material filters match any selected tag. Counts in the drawer reflect the other active filters. Chronology bars select a decade. Every map point, ledger line and label plate opens its record. Ctrl/⌘ + scroll zooms the map; ordinary scrolling moves the page. All data, coastline geometry and the globe renderer are embedded in this HTML.

About this catalogue

Mammoth History Lab · University of Toronto

A catalogue of finds, read alongside their records.

This page presents all {{TOTAL}} rows in {{SOURCE_SHEET}} of {{SOURCE_FILE}} (workbook dated {{SOURCE_DATE}}; built {{BUILD_DATE}}): find sites, discovery years, coordinate provenance and the specimen ages reported in the sources. Workbook values are never changed; display fields are derived and documented.

Reading the symbols

Colour identifies the taxon group. A solid point is a source-stated coordinate, a dashed ring marks an inferred or historical-map position, and a hollow point marks a regional placeholder or an unlocated find. Nearby points are separated on screen only; fine leader lines keep their coordinate anchors.

Citation
